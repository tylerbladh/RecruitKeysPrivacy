RecruitKeys Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

RecruitKeys is an iPhone app and keyboard for saving recruiting messages in folders and inserting them into other apps. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Where your messages are stored

The folders and messages you save are stored on your device and are not sent to us. Depending on your iPhone backup settings, Apple may include this data in a backup. You can delete saved messages and folders in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, but copies may remain in existing backups.

The keyboard

The RecruitKeys keyboard does not ask for "Full Access," so iOS does not allow it to connect to the internet. It does not read or record what you type. It only inserts the text of a message when you tap it. You review and send every message yourself.

Information you put in your messages

Anything you write in your saved messages stays on your device. Please avoid saving sensitive personal details about other people, such as phone numbers or private information, in your message templates.

Apple

If you install RecruitKeys through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect information such as crash reports or usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not control this.

Children

RecruitKeys is intended for recruiting professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above.
