Blade Keys Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Blade Keys, displayed as Blade on your device, is an iPhone app and keyboard for saving message templates and inserting them into other apps. It also offers a shared Blade Library of public message templates.

Your personal messages

Your personal folders and saved messages are stored on your device. The app does not upload them to us or to the shared library. You can edit or delete them in the Messages tab.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include local app data in a backup. Deleting information in the app does not remove copies from existing backups.

The shared Blade Library

When you open the Library tab or refresh it, the app connects to GitHub Pages to download public message templates. It keeps a local copy so the downloaded library can also be used offline and in the keyboard.

These requests do not include your personal saved messages, keyboard search text, or conversation contents. GitHub receives connection information, including your IP address, and logs IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The shared library is public. It is not a place to store private information.

The keyboard

The Blade keyboard does not request Full Access or connect to the internet. It reads your saved messages and the downloaded shared library from storage shared with the Blade app on your device.

The keyboard does not read the contents of your conversations. Text entered into its message search is used locally to find templates and is not saved as search history or transmitted to us.

Tapping a message inserts its text into the app you are using. The keyboard does not send messages. You review and send them yourself. Once inserted, that text is handled by the other app under its own privacy practices.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Blade does not require an account or sign-in. We do not include advertising, tracking tools, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple handles App Store and TestFlight services under its own privacy policies. During TestFlight testing, Apple collects and shares testing information with us, including crash logs, usage information, tester information, and feedback you submit. We use this information to test and improve Blade.

For App Store installations, Apple may share diagnostics with us according to your settings and Apple's policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy and TestFlight & Privacy.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use that information to respond and provide support. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Children

Blade is intended for recruiting professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above.
